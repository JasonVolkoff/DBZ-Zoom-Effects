Drop your own sounds here to replace the built-in ones. Use the same name as the
sound you're replacing, as .mp3 or .wav (.m4a and .aiff work too):

  teleport_out    Instant Transmission: vanishing
  teleport_in     Instant Transmission: reappearing
  kame_charge     Kamehameha: charging
  kame_fire       Kamehameha: the beam (cut off when the beam ends)
  powerup         Aura level 1 power-up (crackles, then the ignition burst at ~2 s)
  powerup_2       Aura level 2 power-up
  powerup_3       Aura level 3 power-up
  aura_loop       Hum while the aura is on (repeats back to back)
  aura_down       Aura dissipating

Example: teleport_out.mp3 replaces the built-in teleport_out.wav.
Changes take effect the next time the sound plays, with no restart needed.
Delete your file to go back to the built-in sound.
